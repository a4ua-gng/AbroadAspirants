Before you go

        
Is it free to join?
Yes. The community and the sample resources on this site are free. Each external program sets its own fees and funding.

        
Are all the programs accepting applications?
Each listing links to the organizer so you can check current deadlines and eligibility.

        
Can a student answer my question?
Use the WhatsApp community link to talk to other students. The Application Desk offers an AI advisor and a quick guide. For peer discussion, use WhatsApp.

        
How should I use a recommendation sample?
Share accurate examples of your work with your recommender. They should write or finalize the letter based on their own experience with you.
